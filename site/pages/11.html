Eight connections per file

Hosts cap each socket, and a busy link is shared per flow. Eight flows claim eight shares. An 83 MiB file, same machine, byte-identical output.

Exact resume

Nothing is lost to a restart

Every finished range is recorded as it lands. Restart the app, the PC or the network and the transfer continues from the byte it stopped at.

HLS → MP4

Streams become one file

Playlists are fetched segment by segment and, with ffmpeg present, written out as a single MP4. Video pages get a button in the extension.
