Eight times the ceiling your browser gives a download.

DM is a download manager for Windows. It opens eight connections to one file, splits the remaining bytes as it goes, resumes exactly after anything, and turns streaming video into MP4. The browser extension hands it the link with your cookies.

Download DM-Setup.exe28 MB. Installs to your user folder, no admin rights.

Hosts cap the socket, not you.

Most servers throttle each connection. Eight connections get eight times the cap. Nothing clever, just more pipes.

A busy link is shared per flow.

TCP divides a bottleneck between flows, not between people. Eight flows claim eight shares and each ramps up on its own.

No slow socket sets the finish.

An idle connection halves the largest remaining range and takes the tail. A slow connection keeps losing territory until the fast ones are done.
