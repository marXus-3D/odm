Speed

Eight connections per file

Hosts cap each socket, and TCP shares a busy link per flow. Ranges split as the transfer runs; every byte is written at its final offset, no part files. 83 MiB: 13.7 s in the browser, 6.5 s here.

Resume

Exact, to the byte

Finished ranges are recorded as they land. Crash, reboot, Wi-Fi drop: dm resume picks up at the byte it stopped at. Streams (.m3u8) come down segment by segment and become one MP4 with ffmpeg.

Ways in

CLI, window, web, browser

dm add, dm ls, dm pause for scripts. A dark native window with a tray icon. The same list on localhost. An extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Vivaldi that hands links over with cookies.
