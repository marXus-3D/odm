13.7 s to 6.5 s on the same file

2.1×faster, byte-identical output

An 83 MiB file from dl.google.com on one connection, then on eight. Most hosts cap each socket, not each person, and TCP gives every flow its own share of a busy link.

Idle at about 9 MB

Written in Go with no third-party modules. It sits in the tray without you noticing it is there.

Resume after anything

Finished ranges are recorded as they land. Restart the app, the PC or the router and it carries on from the same byte.

Streaming video to MP4

HLS playlists are fetched segment by segment and stitched into one MP4 when ffmpeg is installed.

When the queue empties

Open the file or its folder, or have the PC sleep, shut down or restart once the last download is done.

The browser hands downloads over, cookies included

The extension catches a download the moment the browser would start it and passes the link to DM through the browser's own native-messaging channel, with the cookies for that site. Files behind a login download without copying anything by hand.

Video pages get a small button that sends the stream across.

One setup file, and you are done

DM-Setup.exe installs the app to your user folder, registers the browser hook and offers the extension. The same file uninstalls, keeping your download list unless you ask it not to.
