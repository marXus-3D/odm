Connections per file

Hosts cap each socket, not each person. Eight sockets, eight shares of the link. Ranges split as the transfer runs; no part files, no merge.

Exact

Resume after anything

Every finished range is recorded as it lands. App crash, reboot, Wi-Fi drop: the transfer picks up at the byte it stopped at.

MP4

From any stream

HLS playlists come down segment by segment and are written out as one MP4 with ffmpeg. Video pages get a button in the extension.
